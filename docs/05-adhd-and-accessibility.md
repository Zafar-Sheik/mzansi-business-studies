# ADHD, dyslexia & accessibility

ADHD brains thrive on **novelty, quick feedback and clear next steps**, and struggle with walls of text and invisible progress. Dyslexic readers benefit from clear fonts, spacing and hearing text. Business Studies adds its own barrier: long lists of facts in formal language. BusinessMzansi builds support for all of this in – and every setting is in the **Preferences** panel.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| One step at a time | Lessons show one chunk with a clear Next button and a progress bar. |
| Focus mode | Hides navigation so only the lesson is on screen. |
| Focus sprints | A 5/10/15/25-minute timer with movement breaks. |
| Read aloud | Every lesson step and every model part can be read aloud (South African English voice where available), at an adjustable speed. |
| Tappable models | One part at a time instead of a full page of notes; a tick shows what has been explored. |
| Fonts | Inter, Atkinson Hyperlegible or OpenDyslexic; adjustable size and line spacing. |
| Reading ruler | A band that follows the pointer to help keep your place. |
| Word anchors | Bolds the start of each word to guide the eye. |
| Calm mode | Switches off decorative animation (also follows the OS "reduce motion" setting). |
| High contrast | Pure black and white with thick focus rings. |
| Tap alternatives | Every sort, match and sequence activity works with taps and the keyboard – no dragging needed. |
| Forgiving typed answers | Case, hyphens, a leading "the", plural slips and rand formatting (R1 250,50) are all accepted; near-misses get a spelling hint instead of a cross. |
| Extra time | Mock papers offer +25% extra time or untimed practice; the paper planner has an extra-time option. |

## Inclusive by design

- **Lists become activities.** Any list of facts is followed by a sorter, sequence or match, so learners handle the facts instead of only reading them.
- **Memory hooks** (pink callouts) give a mnemonic for every list: the eight Acts, the five BBBEE pillars, the TQM elements, the strategy families.
- **Brain breaks** are written into longer lessons.
- Labs never start moving until the learner presses a button, and each has an *Explain* box in plain language.
- Colour is never the only signal: correct and incorrect states use icons and words as well as colour.
- Feedback is framed as "Not yet", never as failure, and effort earns XP and badges.
- Tables scroll sideways on small screens, and the whole site works on a phone.
