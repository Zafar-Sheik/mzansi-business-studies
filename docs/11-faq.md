# FAQ & troubleshooting

## Does it cover Grade 10 and 11?

No. BusinessMzansi covers the Grade 12 CAPS content that is examined in the NSC papers. Grade 12 papers do draw on some Grade 10 and 11 concepts (for example the business environments); those are explained where they are needed.

## Why are there more facts in the memo than marks?

That is how NSC marking guidelines work: any FOUR correct facts earn 8 marks. Learners tick what they wrote, and the score is capped at the marks for the question.

## How should learners mark themselves?

Strictly. A full fact (statement plus explanation) earns two marks; a heading or half-explained fact earns one in the real exam. Their own wording is fine if the meaning is the same.

## The mock paper is not exactly like a real paper

Correct. The mocks are built from Section B-style questions across all the topics of a paper, so learners practise everything instead of choosing two of three questions. Use the essay planner for Section C and the Section A drills for Question 1.

## A number in a lesson differs from my textbook

Legislation is amended from time to time (thresholds, penalties, leave). The app states provisions as they are generally taught; follow your textbook and the latest examination guidelines, and please report differences.

## Read-aloud does not work

It uses the browser's speech engine. Use a recent version of Chrome, Edge or Safari, and check that the device is not muted.

## Progress disappeared

Progress is stored in the browser. It is lost if site data is cleared, in private windows, or on a different device or browser.

## Can I print?

Yes: worksheets, mock papers and their marking guidelines have print layouts (black on white).
