# Teacher quick-start

Ten minutes to get going.

## 1. Find the lesson for this week

Open **Learn**, choose **Paper 1** or **Paper 2**, then a topic. Every lesson shows its CAPS reference (hover over the CAPS chip) and is split into short steps, so you can use a single step as a 10-minute starter.

## 2. Project a lab or model

Open **Labs**. Nothing moves until you press a button, so labs are safe to project.

- **Act Finder** – read the scenario aloud; the class votes for an Act before you tap.
- **Tappable models** (SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, PESTLE, TQM elements, team stages…) – *Explore* shows one part at a time; *Which one is it?* gives a scenario for the class to identify.
- **Interest lab** and **Insurance lab** – drag the sliders and ask learners to predict the answer before you reveal the working.
- **Essay planner** – build a 40-mark essay together and watch the sub-maximums and the cap of 32 at work.

## 3. Set practice

- **Exam Room** – NSC-style Section B questions per topic. Learners write first, then tick the facts on the marking guideline (two marks for a full fact).
- **Worksheet generator** (Teacher Guide) – printable questions with a memo; the same link always produces the same worksheet.
- **Flashcards** and **Games** – five-minute revision that still counts.

## 4. Write a mock

**Exam Room → Mock papers.** Choose Paper 1 or Paper 2, a full mock (about 150 marks) or a quick mock (about 50), standard time, +25% extra time or untimed. Papers and marking guidelines can be printed.

## 5. Support learners who need it

Show learners the **Preferences** button (top right): one step at a time, focus mode, focus sprints, read-aloud, reading ruler, dyslexia-friendly fonts, calm mode and high contrast. See *ADHD, dyslexia & accessibility*.

## Sharing

There are no accounts. Share a link to any lesson (`/learn/<lesson>`), any topic (`/learn?topic=<topic>`) or any worksheet.
