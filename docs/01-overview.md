# Overview

**BusinessMzansi** is an interactive Grade 12 Business Studies companion for South African learners. It follows the CAPS curriculum and the DBE examination guidelines for the two NSC papers, and it is built so that learners with ADHD, dyslexia and other learning differences can use it comfortably – which makes it easier for everyone.

It is the sister app of MathsMzansi, PhysicsMzansi, EnglishMzansi, AccountingMzansi, LifeMzansi, GeoMzansi and CodeMzansi, with the same learning design and accessibility features.

## What is inside

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lessons** | 24 lessons in 95 short steps (2–12 minutes each) covering every Grade 12 topic in Paper 1 and Paper 2, plus two exam-skills lessons |
| **Labs, models & tools** | 30 entries on the Labs page: Act Finder, 18 tappable models (SWOT, Porter, PESTLE, TQM, team stages…), interest lab, average-clause lab, force-field lab, forms-of-ownership comparison, essay planner and paper planner |
| **Endless practice** | 40 question generators: Section A terms for every topic, 172 scenarios to identify, and interest and average-clause calculations |
| **Flashcards** | 185 key terms with spaced repetition |
| **Quiz Arena** | 96 multiple-choice questions, mixed by topic, timed or relaxed |
| **Exam Room** | 34 NSC-style questions (117 sub-questions, 693 marks) with marking guidelines that learners self-mark |
| **Mock papers** | Paper 1 and Paper 2: a full mock of about 150 marks or a quick mock of about 50, with extra-time and untimed options |
| **Games** | Speed Biz, This or That?, Who Am I?, Concept Pairs, True/False Blitz, Odd One Out, Biz Hangman |
| **Progress** | XP, levels, streaks, badges and a weak-spot coach |

## The two papers

| Paper | Main topics | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| **Paper 1** | Business Environments | Impact of recent legislation · Business strategies · Business sectors and their environments |
| | Business Operations | Human resources function · Quality of performance |
| **Paper 2** | Business Ventures | Management and leadership · Investment: securities · Investment: insurance · Forms of ownership · Presentation and data response |
| | Business Roles | Ethics and professionalism · Creative thinking and problem-solving · Social responsibility and CSR/CSI · Human rights, inclusivity and environmental issues · Team performance and conflict management |

Each paper is 150 marks in 2 hours: Section A (30, compulsory), Section B (80, two of three questions) and Section C (40, one of two essays).

## No accounts, no tracking

Everything runs in the browser. Progress is stored only on the learner's own device.

## A note on accuracy

The content follows the CAPS document and the DBE examination guidelines as closely as possible, and the wording of memo facts follows the style of NSC marking guidelines. Legislation changes: thresholds, leave provisions and penalties are amended from time to time. Always check the latest examination guidelines and your textbook, and treat this app as a study aid, not a legal reference.
