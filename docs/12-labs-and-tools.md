# Labs, models & tools

Everything on the **Labs** page, with ideas for using it in class.

## Tappable models

One explorer renders all 18 models. **Explore** shows one part at a time with a plain explanation, an example and read-aloud. **Which one is it?** shows a clue or scenario and asks the learner to pick the part.

| Paper 1 | Paper 2 | Exam skills |
|---|---|---|
| SWOT analysis · Porter's Five Forces · PESTLE · Strategic management process · Types of business strategies · The three business environments · BBBEE pillars · Consumer rights · Recruitment procedure · Selection procedure · TQM elements · PDCA cycle | Leadership styles · Principles of insurance · Problem-solving steps · Triple bottom line · Stages of team development | LASO |

*In class:* project quiz mode and let groups hold up a card for their answer.

## Act Finder

A scenario appears; tap the Act that applies. A cheat sheet with the purpose and key words of each Act can be switched on. Five in a row earns bonus XP.

## Interest lab

Sliders for the amount, rate and years. Bars show simple and compound interest year by year, and the exam working is written out with the learner's own numbers.

## Insurance lab

Sliders for market value, amount insured and damage. A bar shows who pays for the loss, and the average-clause calculation is shown step by step. Over-insurance and full insurance are explained too.

## Force-field analysis lab

Three proposed changes. Score each driving and restraining force from 1 to 5 and watch the balance tip; the explanation gives the exam steps.

## Forms of ownership: compare two

Pick any two of the eight forms and compare them on the exam's criteria, with factors that contribute to success and to failure.

## Essay planner

Four NSC-style essays (two per paper). Step through introduction, each sub-question, conclusion and LASO, ticking the facts you could write. Shows sub-maximums, the cap of 32 for content and the 8 insight marks.

## Paper planner

Choose the paper, two Section B questions and one essay; get a minute-by-minute plan from 09:00, with an extra-time option.

## Drills

Per main topic: endless scenarios to identify and Section A terms. Plus a calculations drill for interest and the average clause.
