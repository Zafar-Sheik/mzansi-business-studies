# CAPS curriculum map

Grade 12 Business Studies, mapped to the two NSC papers.

## Paper 1 – Business Environments

| Topic | Lessons | Labs & models |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact of recent legislation** – SDA, LRA, EEA, BCEA, COIDA, BBBEE, NCA, CPA: purpose, impact, discriminatory actions, penalties, compliance; SETAs; BBBEE pillars; consumer rights | The SDA & the LRA · The EEA, BCEA & COIDA · BBBEE, the NCA & the CPA | Act Finder · BBBEE pillars · Consumer rights |
| **Business strategies** – strategic management process; SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, PESTLE; integration, intensive, diversification and defensive strategies; strategy evaluation | SWOT, Porter & PESTLE · Types of Strategies & Evaluation | SWOT · Porter · PESTLE · Strategy types · Strategic management process |
| **Business sectors and their environments** – primary, secondary, tertiary; micro, market, macro; extent of control | Business Sectors & Their Environments | The three business environments |

## Paper 1 – Business Operations

| Topic | Lessons | Labs & models |
|---|---|---|
| **Human resources function** – job analysis; recruitment; selection; interviews; employment contract; induction; placement; salary determination; fringe benefits; legislation and HR | Recruitment, Selection & Interviews · Contracts, Induction, Placement & Pay | Recruitment procedure · Selection procedure |
| **Quality of performance** – quality concepts; quality indicators of the eight business functions; TQM elements and their impact on large businesses; PDCA; quality circles; cost of quality | Quality Concepts & Quality Indicators · Total Quality Management | TQM elements · PDCA cycle |

| Lessons | Tools |
